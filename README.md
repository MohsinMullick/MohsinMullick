## 👨‍💻 About Me

I build NLP and machine learning systems, with a focus on **Bangla language** and on making model outputs **reliable and safe** through uncertainty estimation.
I am a Computer Science student at **North South University, Dhaka**, and I do research under faculty supervision.

- 🔭 **Working on:** Bangla medical NER, uncertainty-aware safety checks for LLM outputs, and a Bangla hate speech detection framework
- 🌱 **Learning:** Conformal prediction, multi-agent LLM systems, and competition-style ML workflows
- 🎓 **Next step:** MSc in AI/ML, then research in Bangla and low-resource NLP

**Research interests:** `NLP` · `Bangla NLP` · `Machine Learning` · `Uncertainty Quantification` · `LLM Safety` · `AR Applications`
