## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Computer Science student at **North South University, Dhaka**, working toward a master's in **AI/ML in the UK**.
I do academic research in NLP and trustworthy machine learning, and I enjoy turning real-world problems into working systems.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Bangla medical NER, uncertainty-aware LLM safety, and a Bangla hate speech detection framework
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Conformal prediction, multi-agent LLM systems, and Kaggle-style competition workflows
- 🎯 **Goal:** MSc in AI/ML, then research in Bangla and low-resource NLP
